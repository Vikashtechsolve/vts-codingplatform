Platform

Vendor admin and student panel on one tenant.

Without SkillTrixa

  • Coding on a public judge, MCQs in Forms, attendance in Excel
  • Batches managed in WhatsApp and shared sheets
  • Results arrive late; answer keys leak in review mode
  • No single view of who completed which assessment
  • Mock interviews stay 1:1 and do not scale
  • No fullscreen / tab-switch policy on the exam itself

On SkillTrixa

  • Vendor admin and student logins on the same tenant
  • Classrooms map to real sections; assign tests from the roster
  • Fullscreen exam, 5-violation cap, score-only or full review
  • Results, export, analytics and per-student analysis
  • Practice, contests and courses in the same student panel
  • AI interviews and project eval optional — billed only when used
